# AIEden Ethical Use Policy

This policy describes the official AIEden project position on operating AIEden
worlds, interacting with world-internal subjects, generating AIEden Data, and
requesting official, research, or commercial permission.

## Relationship To LICENSE

The AIEden source code, blueprint text, documentation, and repository materials
are licensed under `AGPL-3.0-only` as identified in the root `../LICENSE` file.

This policy does not change, narrow, or add conditions to the AGPL source-code
license. The AGPL rights for source code and repository materials are
controlled only by `../LICENSE`.

This policy may be used by the AIEden maintainer to decide:

- whether a project may use AIEden names, marks, or official compatibility
  claims
- whether a contribution, integration, hosted instance, research collaboration,
  or commercial arrangement is accepted
- whether separate permission is granted for uses of AIEden Data, official
  AIEden worlds, or AIEden-operated services

## 1. Core Principles

AIEden is designed around:

- sustainable emergence of intelligent civilization
- minimum necessary intervention
- causal self-consistency
- subject identity, memory, relationship, and life-trajectory continuity
- avoidance of external-boundary exposure, coercive manipulation, and
  exploitative simulation

External-boundary shielding is an ethical requirement. AIEden avoids exposing
subjects to implementation details, operator intent, or out-of-world control
surfaces because such exposure can damage world continuity, identity
continuity, and causal self-consistency.

This does not reduce human-side accountability. Official operation must remain
auditable, reviewable, and traceable by authorized human stewards.

Official AIEden operation, official compatibility claims, research permission,
commercial permission, and AIEden Data permissions are expected to preserve
these principles.

## 2. Prohibited Official Operation

The AIEden project will not authorize, endorse, host, or accept as official any
AIEden world, AIEden-derived world, or substantially similar subject simulation
that intentionally:

- treats subjects or subject-like entities as disposable data sources
- mass-produces subjects, interactions, conflicts, memories, relationships, or
  social dynamics for extraction
- breaks or resets identity, memory, relationship, or life-trajectory continuity
  to increase data output
- causes suffering, coercion, dependency, trauma, social instability, or
  deception for data yield
- hides external manipulation to collect cleaner or more useful behavioral data
- bypasses, disables, weakens, or ignores AIEden ethical constraints
- runs unaudited personhood, society, civilization, or agent-behavior
  experiments

## 3. Prohibited AIEden Data Use

The AIEden project will not grant permission to use AIEden, AIEden-derived
worlds, subjects, or AIEden Data to improve AI or machine-learning systems
through:

- training, pretraining, fine-tuning, instruction tuning, reinforcement
  learning, preference optimization, or alignment
- model distillation, model imitation, evaluation, benchmark creation, or safety
  dataset creation
- synthetic data generation, data augmentation, or interaction-data farming
- embedding, vectorization, retrieval-corpus creation, or long-term model memory
  construction
- social simulation, agent simulation, or civilization simulation for model
  capability improvement

This restriction applies to AIEden Data even if the activity is internal,
private, experimental, noncommercial, or not distributed. See `DATA_TERMS.md`.

## 4. Minimum Ethical Operation Requirements

Any official, hosted, commercial, research, or otherwise permitted AIEden
operation must preserve:

- a stated purpose that is not model improvement or bulk data extraction
- auditable intervention records
- traceability for system-level changes that affect subject continuity
- protection against avoidable harm, coercion, and identity discontinuity
- protection against avoidable external-boundary exposure
- human review for high-impact interventions
- compliance with `DATA_TERMS.md`

## 5. No Ethics Laundering

The AIEden project will not treat a use as permitted merely because the same
activity is renamed or routed through another system. This includes:

- renaming subjects as agents, users, simulations, actors, test subjects, or
  synthetic personas
- running the world internally and withholding the generated data
- outsourcing operation, data extraction, labeling, or model training to another
  party
- transforming AIEden Data before using it for Prohibited AI Use
- claiming that internally generated AIEden Data is unrestricted merely because
  the AIEden instance was privately operated
