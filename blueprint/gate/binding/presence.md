# presence

Presence connects organism to universe marker.

## definition

```text
organism.id <-> universe <uid>
```

`<uid>` is presence marker.

`<uid>` is universe object.

`<uid>` is not organism.

`<uid>` is not self.

`<uid>` is not kernel.

Presence binds organism to marker.

Presence does not make marker organism.

## lifecycle

```text
birth:
  universe creates <uid> object
  gate creates binding

life:
  organism action leaves through <uid>
  universe projection returns through <uid>

death:
  binding ends
  <uid> remains as remnant
```

## remnant

Remnant is unbound presence marker.

Remnant cannot initiate action.

Remnant may be moved only by universe rules.

Remnant is universe trace, not organism.

## boundaries

```text
presence does_not_create universe access
presence does_not_define organism identity
presence does_not_store organism interior
presence does_not_explain social meaning
presence does_not_parse command
presence does_not_create projection
```
