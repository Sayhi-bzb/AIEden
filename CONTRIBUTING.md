# Contributing To AIEden

## Legal Model

Contributions to AIEden are accepted under:

```text
community license: AGPL-3.0-only
default inbound contribution terms: AGPL-3.0-only
trademark rights: policy/TRADEMARK.md
commercial licensing: policy/COMMERCIAL.md
official operation policy: policy/ETHICAL_USE_POLICY.md
AIEden Data terms: policy/DATA_TERMS.md
```

By submitting code, documentation, designs, issues, pull requests, patches, or
other material intended for inclusion in AIEden, you agree to submit that
material under the project community license, currently `AGPL-3.0-only`, unless
a separate written agreement applies.

Do not submit material for inclusion unless you have the right to submit it
under those terms.

The source code, blueprint text, documentation, and repository materials are
licensed under `AGPL-3.0-only`. The AGPL rights are controlled by `LICENSE`;
the trademark, data, commercial, and ethical operation documents do not add
extra conditions to the AGPL source-code license.

Contributions intended for commercial relicensing, hosted services, proprietary
distribution, or other special arrangements may require a separate written
agreement before acceptance.

## Contribution Rules

```text
keep changes small
preserve blueprint module boundaries
prefer deleting weak structure over adding new structure
do not add social semantics to universe atoms
do not weaken organism continuity rules
include validation notes for changed public behavior
```
